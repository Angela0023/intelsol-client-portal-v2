USAGE NOTES:
============

1. TIER 1 = Use for ALL companies 50-500 employees
   - This is your PRIMARY target list
   - These are the decision makers with budget authority

2. TIER 2 = Use ONLY for companies 50-100 employees with NO Tier 1 roles
   - Check LinkedIn first: if company has any Tier 1 role, skip Tier 2
   - CEOs/Founders in small companies own culture/benefits decisions

3. TIER 3 = Use ONLY if both Tier 1 AND Tier 2 return ZERO results
   - Last resort targets
   - These are influencers but rarely final decision makers

4. IMPORTANT: In European/Nordic companies, "Manager" titles can be senior
   - "People Manager" or "HR Manager" in a 100-person Danish company might be THE top HR person
   - Don't skip these in your European searches


SEARCH STRATEGY:
================

APOLLO/LINKEDIN SALES NAVIGATOR:
1. Set company size filter: 50-500 employees (100-250 ideal)
2. Set location filter: Denmark (or your target country)
3. Paste Tier 1 job titles in "Job Title" field
4. Review results - if no matches, try Tier 2
5. If still no matches, try Tier 3


PRIORITY ORDER:
1. Tier 1 roles in 100-250 employee companies
2. Tier 1 roles in 50-100 or 250-500 employee companies
3. Tier 2 roles in 50-100 employee companies
4. Tier 3 roles (any company size)


RED FLAGS (DON'T TARGET):
========================
- HR Coordinators, HR Assistants (too junior)
- Recruiters without "Head of" or "Director" title
- Office Managers in companies >200 employees
- Any role in companies >500 employees (out of ICP)
- Any role in companies <50 employees (too small)

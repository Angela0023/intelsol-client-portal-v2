# Intera — ICP Profile

## Company Overview

**Intera (Intrix CRM)** is a B2B sales CRM platform designed for quotation-led sales processes in Croatia and Serbia. It helps sales teams manage complex B2B sales cycles, track quotations, and close deals efficiently.

- **Industry:** Sales Software / CRM
- **Business Model:** B2B SaaS - CRM Platform
- **Target Markets:** Croatia & Serbia
- **Service Type:** Sales CRM with Quotation Management

## What Intrix CRM Offers

- B2B sales CRM platform for quotation-led sales
- Sales pipeline management and tracking
- Quotation management and workflow
- Deal forecasting and analytics
- Integration with existing business systems

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### MUST HAVE Characteristics

1. **Company Size:** 20-250 employees
2. **Location:** Croatia or Serbia
3. **Business Model:** B2B (business-to-business)
4. **Sales Process:** Quotation-led sales (not transaction/e-commerce)
5. **Sales Team:** Multiple sales team members (not solo seller)
6. **Sales Complexity:** Complex sales cycles requiring tracking and management

### Ideal Company Types

- Technical distributors
- Industrial equipment manufacturers
- Building material suppliers
- IT integrators
- Machinery distributors
- Automotive parts suppliers
- Other B2B companies with structured sales processes

### Disqualifiers

- ❌ Under 20 employees
- ❌ Over 250 employees
- ❌ Located outside Croatia and Serbia
- ❌ B2C business model
- ❌ Transaction-based sales (e-commerce only)
- ❌ Already using enterprise CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- ❌ No sales team or solo founder selling

## Email Sequences

Messaging angles aligned with each persona tier:

### Sales Director / Commercial Director

**Angle:** Owns pipeline, quotations and team adoption - needs visibility of opportunities and next actions

Key messaging: Shared view of customers, opportunities and follow-up activities. Keeps pipeline visible without chasing people for updates.

### Managing Director / CEO

**Angle:** Budget sponsor, needs commercial visibility and less dependence on individual knowledge

Key messaging: One shared system for opportunities, quotations and account history. Reduces dependence on information held by individuals.

### Sales Representative / Account Manager

**Angle:** Practical user and champion - needs organized customer records and quotation tracking

Key messaging: One place for customer details, opportunity stages and next actions. Organized follow-up and shared account history.

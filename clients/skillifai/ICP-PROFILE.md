## 1. Company Overview

### What They Sell
Skillifai develops practical AI skills and helps companies introduce AI into business processes through:
- Departmental AI training programmes
- Broader AI adoption and implementation solutions
- Custom projects (EUR 10,000+)

### Target Market
**Primary Focus:** Slovenian SMEs with approximately **20–250 employees** with sufficiently large relevant teams.

## 3. Buying Triggers & Pain Points

### What Triggers a Purchase?
Hypotheses to validate:
- Management launches an AI initiative
- Company purchases AI licences (Copilot, ChatGPT, etc.)
- HR plans employee development
- Internal AI pilot stalls or fails

### Problems They Experience
- Employees use AI inconsistently; lack clear use cases
- Repetitive tasks remain manual despite AI availability
- Pilots don't become part of everyday workflows
- Unused AI licences
- Time lost on documents, reporting, content preparation
- Duplicated work across teams
- Risk exposure in handling data with AI tools

### Business Impact
- Lost time on routine tasks
- Underutilized software investments
- Missed productivity gains
- Compliance and data-handling risks

### Urgency Drivers (To Validate)
- Training planning deadline
- Approved annual budget
- Director/leadership mandate to act
- Failed internal implementation attempts

## 7. Technology & Buying Signals

### Tools & Platforms (Hypotheses to Validate)
- Microsoft 365 / Copilot
- Google Workspace
- ChatGPT
- Business CRM/ERP systems
- Other AI tools

**Note:** No tool is a mandatory filter. Validate technology patterns with best-fit customer data.

### Verified Buying Signals

| Tier | Signal | How to Identify |
|------|--------|-----------------|
| **Tier 1** | Explicit need for AI training | Published request, plan, or expressed interest from decision-maker; record source, date, target team |
| **Tier 1** | Active AI project facing obstacles | Verified pilot/project with stated adoption or implementation problem; licence purchase alone is insufficient |
| **Tier 2** | AI tool or licence rollout | Documented Copilot or similar tool adoption; verify scope and training needs |
| **Tier 2** | Digitalisation or skills development programme | Specific programme, responsible person, training plan; verify current and AI-relevant |
| **Tier 3** | Team growth or leadership change | New hires or new leadership tasked with process improvement; weak signal without additional verified need |

### Where to Find Signals
- LinkedIn profile changes (new hires, promotions)
- Company news (funding, partnerships, AI announcements)
- LinkedIn job postings (training, operations, process improvement roles)
- Company website (digitalisation, AI strategy mentions)
- Industry publications and press releases

## 10. Campaign Strategy & Testing Plan

### Pilot Duration
**3 months from campaign launch**

### Testing Framework
1. **Segment Testing:** By industry (manufacturing vs. logistics vs. professional services)
2. **Persona Testing:** 75% CEO/Directors vs. 25% HR/L&D
3. **Message Testing:** Different angles (cost, risk, opportunity)
4. **Review Cadence:** After 6 weeks, adjust targeting based on response rates, qualification rates, and deal closure

### Key Metrics to Track
- Response rate by persona
- Qualification rate by industry
- Time-to-close by segment
- Customer acquisition cost (vs. EUR 10,000+ project potential)
- Win/loss reasons by persona and industry

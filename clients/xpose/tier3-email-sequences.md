## Email 1A

**Subject:** A different approach than lead generation

**Body:**
```
Most agencies focus on generating more patient inquiries.

What we often see in cosmetic clinics is that the bigger issue is what happens after the inquiry comes in.

Xpose helps clinics build a permanent patient coordination infrastructure inside their existing systems so inquiries are captured, followed up, and converted more consistently without adding pressure to the front desk team.

The goal is not more leads. It is recovering the revenue already being missed operationally.

Happy to show you the operational gaps we typically uncover in cosmetic dermatology clinics if useful.
```

## Email 2 (Follow-Up Day 2)

**Subject:** Re: patient coordination

**Body:**
```
One thing many cosmetic clinics underestimate is how expensive delayed follow-up can become.

In aesthetics, even a missed consultation request can easily mean a lost multi-thousand-dollar procedure.

Most practices do not actually have a demand problem. They have a coordination and response-time problem.

Xpose focuses specifically on fixing that operational layer.
```

---

## Email 3 (Follow-Up Day 4)

**Subject:** Re: patient coordination

**Body:**
```
What usually happens as cosmetic clinics grow is that the front desk becomes the operational bottleneck long before patient demand slows down.

Calls, web inquiries, scheduling, follow-ups, after-hours requests — everything ends up flowing through the same small team.

That is typically where patient leakage starts to happen quietly.

Happy to share how clinics are solving this without adding more operational chaos.
```

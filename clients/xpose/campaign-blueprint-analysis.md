## The "Gap" Research (Your Hook)

**Before outreach, find a specific "leak" = Social Signal for qualified meeting**

### Signal A: Ads but No Chat
- They have active ads on Google AND/OR Meta
- BUT don't have live chat feature on website
- OR enquiry process is clunky

### Signal B: Response Test (Secret Shopper)
- Submit web inquiry
- If no reply in **15 minutes** = Lead Decay
- Track: performed secret shopper test, no response after 1 hour

### Signal C: After-Hours Check
- Call after hours
- Hit voicemail
- NO TEXT received in next 1 hour
- If no "Chat" button = invisible to after-hours patients

### Signal D: The Human Gap
- 200+ reviews
- BUT "broken" booking experience
- Burning reputation

## Qualifying the Meeting ($150 Checklist)

**To get paid for meeting, must ensure:**

1. **Decision Maker:** Talking to MD/Founder OR Practice Manager
2. **Scale:** Clinic has 2+ providers
3. **Proof of Pain:** Identified ONE "Operational Gap"
4. **Padio Entry:** Lead moved to "Discovery Booked" stage with research notes

**Every lead MUST have ONE of these signals attached:**
- ☑ Signal A: Active ads but no live chat/clunky enquiry
- ☑ Signal B: Secret shopper test, no response after 1 hour
- ☑ Signal C: After-hours call, no TEXT in 1 hour

## Tier Structure

### Tier 3 — Broad Campaign (LAUNCH FIRST)
**Goal:** ~1,000 leads, validate messaging quickly

**Personalization:** Minimal
- First name
- Company name

**Messaging:**
- Operational bottlenecks
- Missed inquiries
- Front desk overload
- After-hours leakage
- Patient coordination

**Status:** Launch immediately

---

### Tier 2A — Ads-Based Personalization (NEXT)
**Trigger:** Lead runs Meta Ads OR Google Ads

**Angle:** "You already invest in patient acquisition"

**Core Idea:**
- Clinics already generate demand
- Issue is operational conversion
- Response speed
- Follow-up
- Coordination layer

---

### Tier 2B — Website Friction Personalization (NEXT)
**Trigger:**
- No live chat
- Weak booking flow
- Traditional inquiry forms
- No instant response mechanism

**Angle:** "Modern cosmetic patients expect immediate coordination"

---

### Tier 1 — Manual Personalization (LATER)
**Reserved for:**
- Premium clinics
- Multi-location groups
- Highest-value accounts

**Actions:**
- Booking flow review
- After-hours check
- Secret shopper test
- Loom audit

**Status:** NOT automated initially

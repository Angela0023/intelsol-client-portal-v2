## Usage Notes

**For Small Clinics (5-15 employees):**
- Focus on Tier 1 positions only
- Founder-led practices = target owners/medical directors

**For Larger Clinics (15-25 employees):**
- Include Tier 1 + Tier 2
- Operations managers become more relevant

**Clay/Apollo Search Strategy:**
- Use full comma-separated list in job title filters
- Filter companies first, then find people
- Prioritize Tier 1 titles if multiple matches at same company

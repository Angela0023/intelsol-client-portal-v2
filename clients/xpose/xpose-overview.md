## What They Do

Xpose Solutions specializes in **patient coordination infrastructure** for specialty healthcare clinics. They operate as a systems partner that builds and manages the operational systems between patient inquiry and booked consultation.

### Core Offering: Patient Coordination Engine

A comprehensive system that manages the critical gap between when a patient first shows interest and when they book their first appointment.

**The Problem They Solve:**
Specialty clinics are generating patient inquiries but losing them due to:
- Slow follow-up
- Unclear ownership of coordination tasks
- Disconnected tools
- Booking leakage
- Front desk overwhelm

## Lead Generation Strategy for Xpose

### Ideal Prospect Profile

**Best Fit:**
- Specialty clinics spending on ads/marketing BUT struggling to convert inquiries to appointments
- Founder-led practices feeling "busy but chaotic"
- Clinics with existing CRM/tools but poor utilization
- Practices losing revenue to booking leakage
- **Clinics currently running paid ads** (Google Ads, Facebook Ads, TikTok Ads) - this is a key qualification signal

### Red Flags (Poor Fit)
- Clinics without existing inquiry volume (not spending on ads/marketing)
- Practices looking for marketing/lead generation services (they need coordination, not more leads)
- General practitioners (not specialty clinics)
- Organizations wanting to replace all existing systems (Xpose works alongside existing tools)

## EMAIL SEQUENCE STRATEGY

**Sequence Structure:** Position-based

**Number of Sequences:** 2

**Sequence A: Operational & Technical**
- **Targets:** Procurement Manager (TIER 1), Packaging Manager (TIER 2), Supply Chain Director (TIER 3)
- **Messaging Angle:** Evaluate Stojkov as your nearshore carton partner: cost comparison, technical feasibility, production capacity, delivery reliability, quality consistency (ISO/FSC certified)
- **Decision Criteria:** These personas evaluate based on specs, cost, capacity, technical fit, and delivery reliability
- **Email Focus:** "Here's how we meet your production requirements"
- **Key Message:** Cost savings, capacity, technical breadth, nearshore advantage

**Sequence B: Strategic & Brand**
- **Targets:** CEO/Owner/Managing Director (TIER 4), Brand Manager (TIER 5), Quality/Design Manager (TIER 6)
- **Messaging Angle:** Partner with Stojkov for premium branded packaging: family-owned reliability, European manufacturing partner, competitive nearshore economics, quality certifications, design and programme support
- **Decision Criteria:** These personas evaluate based on partnership fit, long-term reliability, brand alignment, and quality standards
- **Email Focus:** "Here's why we're the right long-term partner"
- **Key Message:** Reliability, partnership, quality, brand fit, design support

**Rationale:**
Stojkov serves multiple industries (Cosmetics, Food, Pharma, Publishing, Gifting) but the **procurement journey is fundamentally similar across all industries**. The buying decision differs by ROLE, not by industry:
- Operational buyers care about specs, cost, capacity, delivery
- Strategic buyers care about partnership, reliability, brand fit, and long-term fit

Therefore, **Position-based (2 sequences)** is more effective than Industry-based, reducing complexity while improving conversion rates through role-specific messaging.

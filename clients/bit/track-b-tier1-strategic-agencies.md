## Prospecting Signals

- Salesforce or Adobe partner that needs to scale SFCC or Magento dev capacity
- Availability of an enterprise retainer, but lacking the certified technical depth
- Expanding its geographic footprint from the USA to Europe or vice versa
- Recently lost a senior developer or had a team reduction
- Listed and active on the Salesforce AppExchange

## Persona 1: VP of Delivery / Engagement Director

**Industry:** Full-service Digital Agency
**Geography:** USA

**Goals:**
- Deliver client projects on time and on budget without burning the internal team
- Access certified SFCC and Magento talent fast, without a long recruitment process
- White-label development at its finest, the client is managed by the agency

**Pain Points:**
- Cannot find reliable nearshore or offshore partners that match the agency quality bar
- Previous partners missed deadlines and damaged client relationships
- Timezone gaps cause review and approval delays that compound into larger problems

**Buying Triggers:**
- Winning a new enterprise client requiring SFCC development
- Internal development team overloaded with concurrent projects
- Existing nearshore or offshore partner failed before on a key delivery

**What Makes Persona Say Yes:**
- NDA and white-label capability demonstrated upfront
- Sample code and technical audit demonstrating quality standards
- Discovery call with Bee IT's delivery to establish technical credibility (once it fulfills at least BANT qualification)

## Persona 2: CEO / Managing Director (Boutique SFCC Agency)

**Industry:** Salesforce Commerce Cloud System Integrator
**Geography:** Germany

**Goals:**
- Win larger SFCC enterprise deals by adding flexible dev capacity
- Improve project margins by leveraging outsourcing talent
- Build a long-term, trusted sub-contractor relationship with consistent quality

**Pain Points:**
- Western European agencies charge rates that make enterprise projects uncompetitive
- Hard to find SFCC-certified developers in Western Europe
- Clients increasingly compare agency-built solutions to best-in-class alternatives

**Buying Triggers:**
- New SFCC project in the pipeline that exceeds current team capacity
- Expansion into new industry verticals requiring fresh expertise
- Existing sub-contractor raises rates or underperforms

**What Makes Persona Say Yes:**
- Shared list of Bee IT SFCC certifications and case studies
- High-level rates (indicative) showing a clear cost advantage vs Western European alternatives
- A reference from another EU-based agency already using Bee IT

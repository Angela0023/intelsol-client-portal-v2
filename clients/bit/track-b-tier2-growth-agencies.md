## Persona: Founder / CEO (Boutique eCommerce Agency)

**Industry:** eCommerce Consultancy
**Geography:** Netherlands

**Goals:**
- Scale project delivery without growing full-time headcount
- Take on Magento Enterprise clients that are currently out of reach
- Improve delivery margins on the existing project portfolio

**Pain Points:**
- A small team gets overwhelmed when multiple large projects run concurrently
- Cannot find reliable Magento developers locally at competitive rates
- Clients are pushing for faster delivery windows than the team can support

**Buying Triggers:**
- New client wins a project requiring Magento Enterprise scope
- Current sub-contractor raises rates or fails a delivery
- Bee IT was introduced via an industry contact or mutual connection

**What Makes Persona Say Yes:**
- Demonstrated portfolio overlap, Bee IT has built similar things for similar clients
- Flexible engagement model: T&M or fixed price, depending on the project
- A low-stakes pilot project to prove quality within two weeks

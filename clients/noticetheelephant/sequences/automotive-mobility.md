# Notice the Elephant — Automotive and Mobility Sequence

**Target positions:** Marketing directors, digital leaders, product communication managers
**Tone:** Professional, consultative
**Focus:** Product communication, market expansion, digital presence

---

## Email 1 — Initial Outreach

**Subject:** Marketing at {{company_name}}

---

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm trying to find the right person at {{company_name}} responsible for digital marketing and product communication.

At Notice the Elephant, we help companies with marketing campaigns, content and websites.

Would that be you, or could you point me to the right person?

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Email 2 — First Follow-up

Hi {{first_name}},

Just following up on my note below.

Do you handle this at {{company_name}}, or would a colleague be better to contact?

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Email 3 — Final Follow-up

Hi {{first_name}},

One last note from me. Is support with upcoming campaigns or product launches something your team is considering?

If it's not a priority, no problem.

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Campaign Notes

- **Optimal spacing:** 3-4 days between Email 1 and Email 2, 7 days between Email 2 and Email 3
- **Entry segment:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, company website research
- **Verification:** Confirm active marketing or product communication function before sending
- **Reference point:** Product launches, international expansion announcements

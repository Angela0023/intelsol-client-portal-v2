# Notice the Elephant — Website Redesign Sequence

**Target positions:** Marketing directors, IT directors, operations managers, website owners
**Tone:** Professional, consultative
**Focus:** Website strategy, conversion optimization, project planning

---

## Email 1 — Initial Outreach

**Subject:** Website at {{company_name}}

---

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm looking for the person at {{company_name}} responsible for the company website.

At Notice the Elephant, we help companies plan and redesign their websites, from content and structure through to delivery.

Would that be you, or someone else on the team?

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Email 2 — First Follow-up

Hi {{first_name}},

Just following up to check who looks after website projects at {{company_name}}.

Would you be the right person to ask?

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Email 3 — Final Follow-up

Hi {{first_name}},

Is a website redesign something you're considering over the coming months?

If it's not on the agenda, happy to leave it here.

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Campaign Notes

- **Optimal spacing:** 3-4 days between Email 1 and Email 2, 7 days between Email 2 and Email 3
- **Entry segment:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator, company website analysis (WordPress, Shopify, etc. detection)
- **Verification:** Confirm website or digital strategy ownership before sending
- **Reference point:** Announced redesign plans, outdated website indicators, new platform migrations, complex multi-market/multi-language needs
- **Opportunity criteria:** Projects around EUR 50,000+, complex offers, multiple languages or markets, integration requirements, clear conversion goals

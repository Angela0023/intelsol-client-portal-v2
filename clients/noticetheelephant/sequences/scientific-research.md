# Notice the Elephant — Scientific and Research-Intensive Sequence

**Target positions:** Communications directors, marketing leaders, product managers, R&D communications specialists
**Tone:** Professional, consultative, technically aware
**Focus:** Complex communication, stakeholder messaging, research communication

---

## Email 1 — Initial Outreach

**Subject:** Communications at {{company_name}}

---

Hi {{first_name}},

I'm trying to connect with the person at {{company_name}} responsible for communicating your products and research to customers and partners.

At Notice the Elephant, we help organisations turn complex scientific topics into clear content and digital communication.

Would you be the right person, or could you point me in the right direction?

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Email 2 — First Follow-up

Hi {{first_name}},

Following up on my note below.

Does product and research communication fall under your team, or would someone else be better to contact?

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Email 3 — Final Follow-up

Hi {{first_name}},

Are you considering any outside support with content or communication around upcoming products or research projects?

If it's not relevant at the moment, no problem.

Best,
{{sender_name}}

---

## Campaign Notes

- **Optimal spacing:** 3-4 days between Email 1 and Email 2, 7 days between Email 2 and Email 3
- **Entry segment:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator (pharma, biotech, medtech, deep tech), company research databases
- **Verification:** Confirm R&D communication or product communication function before sending
- **Reference point:** Product launches, research milestones, regulatory approvals, partnership announcements, hiring for communication roles
- **Tone calibration:** This segment values scientific accuracy and sophisticated communication - adjust tone accordingly

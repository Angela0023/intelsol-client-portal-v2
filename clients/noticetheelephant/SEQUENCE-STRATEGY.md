**Assignee:** Frosina
**Date:** November 2026
**Status:** Active

---

## Strategy Overview

Notice the Elephant uses **segment-based email sequences** (4 sequences total), each tailored to a specific business vertical and buyer motivation.

This approach recognizes that Notice the Elephant's value proposition varies significantly by industry and buyer context:
- **Automotive/Mobility:** Focus on product communication and market expansion
- **Sports/Ecommerce:** Focus on sales optimization and audience engagement
- **Scientific/R&D:** Focus on complex communication and stakeholder messaging
- **Website Redesign:** Focus on strategic website planning and conversion optimization

---

## Sequence Structure

### Email Sequence Overview

| Segment | Target Positions | Focus | Emails |
|---------|-----------------|-------|--------|
| **Automotive & Mobility** | Marketing Director, Digital Leader, Product Communications Manager | Product communication, market expansion, digital presence | 3 |
| **Commercial Sports & Ecommerce** | Marketing Director, Ecommerce Manager, Event Director | Campaign effectiveness, sales optimization, audience engagement | 3 |
| **Scientific & Research-Intensive** | Communications Director, Marketing Leader, Product Manager, R&D Comms | Complex communication, stakeholder messaging, research communication | 3 |
| **Website Redesign** | Marketing Director, IT Director, Operations Manager | Website strategy, conversion optimization, project planning | 3 |

### Email Sequence Template

Each sequence follows this 3-email structure:

1. **Email 1 (Initial):** Introduction, identify decision maker, clear CTA (20-min call or permission to share observations)
2. **Email 2 (Follow-up):** Soft confirmation of right person, check if worth continuing
3. **Email 3 (Final):** Gentle, final outreach asking if this is a current priority, offer to step back

**Spacing:** 3-4 days (Email 1 → Email 2), 7 days (Email 2 → Email 3)

## Decision Logic: Segment Selection

### Automotive and Mobility
**Who to target:**
- Established vehicle/component manufacturers
- Automotive suppliers with international sales
- Aftermarket product businesses
- Mobility technology providers

**Entry criteria:**
- Company size: 50+ employees
- International sales or expansion ambitions
- Evidence of product complexity (websites, materials, announcements)
- No Carglass Italia or direct competitors

**Buying signals:**
- Product launch announcements
- International market entry announcements
- New marketing leadership appointments
- Website redesign announcements

### Commercial Sports and Sports Ecommerce
**Who to target:**
- Commercial event organisers (sports events with ticket/registration revenue)
- Professional sports clubs with marketing budgets
- Sports apparel or equipment brands with ecommerce

**Entry criteria:**
- Commercial focus (not amateur or volunteer)
- Meaningful marketing/advertising budget
- Clear registration, ticket, or product sales objective
- Evidence of digital marketing activity

**Buying signals:**
- Upcoming event announcements
- Seasonal campaign periods
- New product launch announcements
- Sponsorship or partnership announcements

### Scientific and Research-Intensive
**Who to target:**
- Pharmaceutical, biotech, medtech companies
- Deep tech and engineering firms
- Research-intensive industrial companies (with proprietary products)

**Entry criteria:**
- Proprietary products or active R&D
- Multiple stakeholder audiences (customers, partners, employees)
- Evidence of complex product/process
- Revenue-generating business model (not pure research)

**Buying signals:**
- Product/patent announcements
- Research milestone announcements
- Regulatory approval announcements
- Hiring for communication/marketing roles
- Partnership announcements

### Website Redesign
**Who to target:**
- Medium-sized B2B companies (50-500 employees)
- Ecommerce businesses
- Any company in target segments considering substantial website project

**Entry criteria:**
- Project budget: EUR 50,000+
- Complex offer (multiple products/services)
- Multi-language or multi-market presence
- Clear integration or conversion requirements

**Buying signals:**
- Website redesign announcements
- Outdated website indicators (technology stack analysis)
- Platform migration announcements
- New market entry announcements
- Product line expansion announcements

## Targeting Approach

### Primary Sources
1. **LinkedIn Sales Navigator:** Search by title, company size, industry, location
2. **Company websites:** Identify decision makers, verify business model
3. **Industry directories:** Automotive, sports, pharma, research sectors
4. **Event lists:** Trade shows, conferences, exhibitor lists
5. **Public announcements:** Press releases, news coverage, funding announcements

### Qualification Checklist

Before adding a prospect to any sequence:

- [ ] Company fits segment definition?
- [ ] Company is in Northern Italy or demonstrates cross-border/Italian interest?
- [ ] Decision maker identified and title verified?
- [ ] Revenue range suggests adequate budget?
- [ ] No exclusion applies?
- [ ] Not an existing client or active opportunity?
- [ ] Relevant buying signal or announcement found?
- [ ] Company verified (legal registration, actual operations)?

## Campaign Management

### List Building
- **Target:** 50-100 prospects per segment per month (200-400 total monthly outreach)
- **Refresh:** Add new prospects continuously to maintain pipeline
- **Deduplication:** Check across all segments to avoid duplicate outreach

### Campaign Tracking
- **Sequence start:** Record date and email 1 send date
- **Follow-up emails:** Send per schedule (3-4 days, then 7 days)
- **Replies:** Log all replies, hand off to sales for response
- **Unresponsive:** Mark sequence as complete after email 3, may retry in 90 days

### Response Management
- **Positive reply:** Hand off to Frosina with full prospect context
- **"Not interested" reply:** Respect bounce, mark as unqualified
- **Position change reply:** Update records, may retry with new contact
- **No reply:** Mark sequence complete after email 3

## Sequence Variations (Testing)

### Planned A/B Tests

**Test 1: Direct value prop vs. soft introduction**
- **Variant A (current):** Soft introduction with minimal value prop
- **Variant B:** Include one sentence value proposition in Email 1
- **Metric:** Open rate, reply rate, meeting booked rate

**Test 2: Email length**
- **Variant A (current):** Very short (2-3 sentences per email)
- **Variant B:** Slightly longer (4-5 sentences) with specific reference
- **Metric:** Reply rate, qualification quality

**Implementation:** Run tests segment-by-segment, volume equal across variants

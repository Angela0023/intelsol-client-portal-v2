# Notice the Elephant — ICP Summary

**Document date:** November 2026
**Assignee:** Frosina

## Agency Overview

Notice the Elephant is a full-service marketing agency combining strategy, digital campaigns, content, websites and conversion optimisation across the customer journey. The agency brings these activities together rather than selling isolated posts or ads.

**Geography:** Northern Italy
**Target:** Established private companies with measurable growth needs, stronger digital presence, or clear communication of complex products and research

---

## Target Company Profile

- **Regional focus:** Northern Italy
- **Revenue range:** To be confirmed per prospect
- **Employee range:** Established businesses (typically 50+)
- **Buying signals:** Website redesign projects, campaign launches, new market entry, product launches
- **Decision makers:** Marketing leaders, digital directors, ecommerce managers, C-level executives

---

## Priority Segments

### 1. Automotive and Mobility
**Who:** Vehicle and component manufacturers, suppliers, aftermarket product businesses, mobility technology providers

**Relevant needs:**
- Presenting complex products to customers and partners
- Entering international markets
- Supporting a launch
- Improving corporate website or lead generation journey

**Strong fit:**
- Established business with own marketing function
- International sales ambitions
- Need for strategic and delivery support

**Exclusion:** Carglass Italia and direct Carglass competitors in Italy (automotive glass repair/replacement). Hold ambiguous cases for agency review.

---

### 2. Commercial Sports and Sports Ecommerce
**Who:**
- Commercial organisers of larger sporting events
- Professional clubs with suitable marketing budgets
- Sports apparel or equipment brands with own online sales

**Relevant needs (Events):**
- Registrations and ticket sales campaigns
- Audience engagement initiatives

**Relevant needs (Ecommerce):**
- Product sales and purchase journey optimization
- Campaign effectiveness for seasons and collections

**Strong fit:**
- Commercial organisation with meaningful budget
- Clear sales objective (registrations, tickets, product sales)
- Need to connect advertising, content, email, and website

**Poor fit:** Small clubs or events without commercial budget

---

### 3. Private Scientific and Research Intensive Businesses
**Who:**
- Pharmaceutical, biotech, medtech companies
- Deep tech and engineering firms
- Research-intensive industrial companies

**Relevant needs:**
- Explaining complex solutions
- Communicating innovation to customers, partners, or potential employees
- Reaching multiple stakeholder audiences
- Scientifically accurate content delivered across channels

**Strong fit:**
- Proprietary products or R&D capabilities
- Multiple stakeholder audiences (customers, partners, employees)
- Need for scientifically accurate content across channels

**Poor fit:** Public universities and research institutes (unless with specific commercial objectives)

---

### 4. Website Redesign Opportunity
**Target:** Medium-sized B2B and ecommerce businesses outside core segments

**Requirements:**
- Substantial redesign project (~EUR 50,000+)
- Complex offer, multiple languages or markets
- Integrations required
- Clear conversion requirements

## Needs and Buying Signals

### Problems the Agency Can Help Address

1. **Website clarity problem:** Website does not clearly explain the offer or support enquiries and sales
2. **Disconnected martech:** Advertising, content, CRM, and website activity are disconnected
3. **Capacity problem:** Internal team lacks capacity for larger project
4. **Event/retail revenue:** Event or retailer seeking more registrations, tickets, or product sales
5. **Communication problem:** R&D business struggling to explain complex expertise to different audiences

*Note: These are conversation hypotheses. Do NOT claim unverified problems or lost revenue at a specific company.*

### Signals to Prioritize

- **Product/service launches:** New product announcements, market entries
- **Website updates:** Recent website redesigns or announced redesign plans
- **Campaign announcements:** New campaign initiatives or marketing announcements
- **Team changes:** New marketing/digital leadership appointments
- **Funding/growth signals:** Investment rounds, expansion announcements
- **Supplier relationships:** Strategic supplier appointments (may indicate project planning)

**Always verify:** Get source link, date, and factual description. Do NOT use unverified technology claims.

### Technology Context (Supporting Only)

**Website platforms:** WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, Magento, Adobe Commerce, Drupal, Webflow

**Advertising/measurement:** Google Ads, Meta Ads, GA4, Google Tag Manager

**CRM/automation:** HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, Klaviyo

*Note: Technology alone does NOT qualify a prospect or prove its setup works.*

## Campaign Approach

### Tone
- **Language:** Concise, professional, consultative Italian
- **Personalization:** One verified observation about the company
- **Connection:** Clear link to the agency's offering
- **CTA:** One clear call-to-action per message

### Primary CTA
20-minute introductory conversation to understand needs

### Alternative CTAs
- "Is this a priority for your team in the coming months?"
- "Can I share two or three observations about your situation?"

### Copy Standards
- ✅ Use verified observations from research
- ✅ Have Italian copy reviewed by native speaker with industry knowledge
- ✅ Professional tone, consultative approach
- ❌ Avoid generic social media pitches
- ❌ Don't claim guaranteed results
- ❌ Don't invent company problems
- ❌ Don't make unverified technology claims
- ❌ Don't imply Carglass work or frequent filming as standard

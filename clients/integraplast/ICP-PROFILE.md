# Integraplast ICP Profile

**Last Updated:** October 16, 2026

## Company Overview
Integraplast supplies and services injection-moulding equipment, automation and process solutions for plastics manufacturers in Slovenia and Croatia. The offer combines TEDERIC machines with local and regional technical and after-sales support.

## Target Geography
**Slovenia and Croatia ONLY** — The relevant injection-moulding plant or planned production investment must be in one of these two countries.

## Target Company Size
- **Range:** 10–500 employees
- **Sweet Spot:** 20–250 employees
- **Note:** Revenue is not a hard filter; production scale, technical requirements and investment readiness matter more.

## Primary Segments (Ranked by Priority)

### Segment A — Contract Injection Moulders & Technical Plastics (PRIORITY 1)
Companies producing moulded components for external customers, with an established machine fleet or a defined expansion project.

### Segment B — Automotive Tier 1 & Tier 2 Suppliers
Manufacturers that mould automotive plastic components internally (interior parts, housings, connectors, lighting, electrical or EV-related components).

### Segment C — Plastic Packaging Manufacturers
Injection-moulded containers, buckets, tubs, caps and closures, thin-wall packaging and IML packaging. Focus on additional capacity, repeatability, fast cycles.

### Segment D — Household & Consumer Products
Manufacturers with in-house injection moulding: storage products, bins, kitchen and garden products, furniture components.

### Segment E — Medical/Electronics/Precision Plastics
Secondary, potentially high-value manufacturers in medical, electronics, electrical, construction, and precision or technical plastics. Fit depends on internal moulding or concrete plan to establish it.

## Business Models
- Contract moulding operations
- OEM or own-brand manufacturing with internal moulding
- Integrated tooling-plus-moulding businesses
- Both established production and concrete new injection-moulding investment

## Exclusion Criteria (DO NOT CONTACT)
- Companies that only buy plastic parts (no internal moulding)
- Pure plastics distributors or resellers
- Tooling-only businesses without moulding production
- Very small hobby or micro operations
- Extrusion-only or blow-moulding-only manufacturers (unless they also injection mould)
- Recycling-only companies
- Businesses with neither evidence nor realistic plan for injection moulding

## Buying Signals (Prioritized by Urgency)

### TIER 1: Defined Capacity or Equipment Investment
New moulding line, planned machine purchase or replacement, or a new facility with confirmed injection-moulding capacity.

### TIER 1: Committed Project or Capacity Need
New automotive, FMCG or industrial contract; constrained moulding capacity; or new product requiring different tonnage, process or multi-component capability.

### TIER 2: Automation or Energy-Efficiency Initiative
Planned cell-automation project, hydraulic-machine modernization or energy-reduction initiative tied to moulding.

### TIER 2: Specialist Hiring or Expansion
Moulding, process, plastics or tooling roles; broader plant expansion where equipment requirement not yet explicit.

### TIER 3: Established Injection-Moulding Operation
Confirmed machines or moulding production, including named machine brands, but no current investment trigger.

- **Machine Brands:** Engel, Arburg, KraussMaffei, Haitian, Wittmann Battenfeld, Fanuc, Sumitomo/SHI Demag
- **Hiring Signals:** Injection Moulding Technicians, Process Engineers, Plastics Engineers, Tooling Engineers
- **Project Signals:** Automation projects and energy-efficiency initiatives linked to moulding

## Value Proposition
Modern injection-moulding technology with local and regional technical support, configured around the application and the complete production cell.

### Key Positioning Angles
1. **Modernization and Energy:** Replace older hydraulic machines with potential for 30–80% lower energy consumption
2. **Capacity and New Projects:** Add capacity efficiently or support upcoming products/contracts with required tonnage, shot size and multi-component capability
3. **Precision and Automation:** Improve repeatability with robot handling, material preparation, temperature control and process optimization
4. **Service and Implementation:** Local Slovenian office, warehouse and service presence with regional technical support

## Preferred Call to Action
"Would a short technical discussion be useful to review your current injection-moulding setup, planned capacity investments or an upcoming project?"

## Communication Tone
Technical, consultative and credible. Lead with the production requirement and a relevant question. Use clear application language and realistic benefits, without aggressive selling or unsupported performance claims.

# PACIA ICP Profile

**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

## Company Overview
PACIA designs bespoke AI customer support agents using a company's own knowledge, with multilingual support across web, email and social channels. Delivery includes knowledge preparation, brand adaptation, testing and deployment, with approximately 4-week implementation timeline. PACIA is positioned as an implementation partner for businesses that need a practical way to reduce repetitive support work.

## Target Geography
**Slovenia (Primary Market)**
— No UK or US expansion until local validation shows repeatable accepted opportunities from specific segments and PACIA has capacity to serve another market.

## Target Company Size
- **Range:** 20–250 employees
- **Priority Band:** 30–150 employees
— Within the range, prioritise 30–150 as likely to have established support workload while retaining relatively direct buying process. Keep 20–29 and 151–250 eligible.

## Core Value Proposition
Help the support team spend less time on recurring questions while making routine information easier for customers to access. Start with one clearly defined category of customer questions using approved information, then assess broader coverage after quality and operational fit are established.

## Primary Target Industries

### TIER 1: E-commerce & Retail (60% allocation)
Slovenian online retailers, consumer brands and established retailers with active online sales channel. Focus on recurring delivery, returns, product and payment-policy enquiries.

**Pain:** Repeat questions about delivery, returns and products consume support time.
**Value Message:** Reduce routine handling and improve access to approved information.

### TIER 2: Tourism & Hospitality (40% allocation)
Hotel groups, larger accommodation operators, tour operators and travel businesses with visible guest enquiry activity. Focus on recurring booking information, guest policies, languages and enquiry coverage around peak periods.

**Pain:** Repeat guest questions and multiple languages stretch reception or reservations teams.
**Value Message:** Improve enquiry coverage and staff capacity during busy periods.

### TIER 3: Second-Wave Segments (Post-validation)
- **Logistics:** Delivery, fulfilment and transport providers (advance when information use case separable from live tracking integrations)
- **Service Businesses:** Established providers with standard offerings, booking or appointment processes and repeat customer questions
- **Subscription:** Recurring consumer or business services with onboarding and account enquiries

## Operational Fit Criteria

Companies must demonstrate ALL of these characteristics:

1. **Customer Interaction** — Active support or guest enquiry function visible or confirmed
2. **Repeatability** — Common questions answerable from stable, approved information
3. **Business Pressure** — Growth, seasonal peaks, team workload, response gaps or new languages create reason to act
4. **Knowledge Readiness** — Policies, FAQs, product or service information exist with owner willing to review
5. **Buying Readiness** — Relevant manager open to discovery and can involve sponsor or budget holder
6. **Technical Scope** — Useful first release feasible without assuming unsupported integrations
7. **Economic Fit** — Prospect can describe operational value justifying a scoped paid project

## Best Fit Account Profile

**Strongest fit:** Established Slovenian business with recurring customer enquiries, a clear owner of the support process and usable company information. Needs to improve support capacity, coverage or consistency and is willing to start with a defined scope. Employee count is a sourcing proxy; a real support problem and viable implementation matter more than size alone.

### Contact Strategy
- Research up to **2 relevant contacts per account**
- Start with **support or operations owner** where identifiable; otherwise CEO or owner
- Contact **one person at a time**
- If no response, approach second contact after **5 business days**
- Any reply pauses other outreach to the account while conversation is assessed

## Buying Signals (Prioritized by Evidence Quality)

### Confirmed Signal Categories
- **Support Recruitment:** Current job advert URL with publication date (evidence of capacity planning and recurring workload)
- **New Market or Language:** Company announcement or active localised website (how support will cover new customers)
- **Seasonal Demand:** Published operating season, offer or event (how enquiry volume is handled at peak times)
- **Multiple Support Channels:** Contact page with active customer-facing channels (which enquiries repeat across channels)
- **Response Complaints:** Specific public review mentioning slow response with date (validate in discovery; ask about response processes without asserting poor service)

## Negative ICP (Exclusions)

**DO NOT CONTACT:**
- Businesses with little recurring support activity or enquiries that are almost entirely bespoke and expert-led
- Prospects seeking only the cheapest self-service chatbot, unwilling to invest in implementation or maintaining accurate information
- Companies without access to usable knowledge, an accountable internal owner or permission to use required systems
- Projects demanding autonomous decisions or transactions beyond PACIA's confirmed capability, without a feasible narrower first release
- Large procurement-led projects outside core size range unless PACIA explicitly accepts sales and delivery requirements
- Existing PACIA customers (suppression takes precedence)
- Active sales opportunities and partners
- Opt-outs and hard bounces
- Direct competitors

## Messaging and Campaign Framework

### Message Structure
Use a verified company observation, a relevant support hypothesis, a brief description of PACIA and a single discovery call to action. Write in clear Slovenian for the local launch, with English as the internal working language.

### E-commerce Angle
Help the support team handle routine delivery, returns and product questions with less repetitive work. The first conversation establishes which enquiry category is worth automating.

### Tourism Angle
Make recurring guest information easier to access across languages and busy periods. Start with information support before discussing booking-system actions.

### Email Template (Master Copy in English)
```
Subject: Customer enquiries at [Company]

Hi [First name],

I noticed [verified company observation].

How does your team currently handle recurring questions about [relevant category]?

PACIA builds tailored AI support around a company's own information.

Would a short conversation about whether this could fit your support process be useful?

[Approved sender]
```
*Note: Localise to Slovenian for actual outreach. Observation and question must be specific to the selected segment.*

### Qualification Definition
An account is qualified for PACIA when a relevant contact confirms a concrete support issue, expresses interest in evaluating a solution and agrees to a discovery conversation or specific next step. Record likely use case, approximate scale if disclosed, decision role and timing. Budget may be unknown at this stage.

# CleverCraft - ICP Profile

## Company Overview
CleverCraft sells a business orchestration platform that unifies backend data, powers flexible API delivery, and enables headless UIs to drive next-generation partner experiences. By decoupling backend complexity from the front end, they unify fragmented systems so enterprise leaders can meet partners where they want to be met, on any channel, platform, or interface.

## Primary Buyers
- Partner Experience Leaders
- Partner Programs Managers
- Partner Operations Leaders
- Sales Operations Directors
- eCommerce/Digital Transformation Directors

## Target Geography
- North America (primary)
- Europe (equal weight)

## Target Company Size
- **Enterprise:** Revenue >$1B
- **Mid-market:** Revenue $100M+
- **Focus:** Mature companies with aging/legacy systems facing foundational system changes

## Target Industries
**Primary:**
- IT Channel (non-vendors only)
- Value-Added Resellers (VARs) & Solution Providers
- Distributors
- Managed Service Providers (MSPs)
- Equipment & Machinery Manufacturers
- Private Equity (Buy & Build / Rollup strategies)

## ⚠️ CRITICAL EXCLUSIONS
**DO NOT TARGET:**
- ❌ Technology vendors (IBM, Oracle, HP, HPE, AWS, etc.)
- ❌ IT consulting and services firms (Cognizant, EPAM, etc.)
- ❌ Outsourcing/BPO companies (Concentrix, Crossover, etc.)

**Reason:** Client explicitly excludes all IT and consulting companies. CleverCraft builds platforms FOR vendors; they target the channel intermediaries (distributors, resellers, VARs), not the vendors themselves.

## Buying Signals (Tier 1 = Strongest)
**Tier 1:**
- M&A activity (distributor/reseller rollups with multiple ERPs/CRMs)
- Partners reselling/distributing from major vendors (where CleverCraft understands the data architecture)
- Channel program changes or new program launches
- Foundational system changes (CRM, ERP replacements)
- Leadership changes in Partner Experience, Channel, or Sales Ops

**Tier 2:**
- Manual order handling/intake
- Local branches with independent systems and P&L
- Legacy system modernization initiatives

**Tier 3:**
- Factory order desks handling custom configurations via email

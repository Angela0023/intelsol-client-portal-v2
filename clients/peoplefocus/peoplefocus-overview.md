## What They Do

People Focus is an **IT & Executive Recruitment agency** specializing in connecting DACH-based companies (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) with senior IT talent from Serbia and the Balkans.

### Core Service
- Sourcing senior developers, software engineers, and IT managers
- Recruiting executives for DACH companies
- Access to 4,000+ active candidates in their database
- 1,000+ candidate interviews per year
- 20+ years of management experience

### Target Market
- DACH companies (Germany, Austria, Switzerland)
- Companies with 50-1,000 employees
- B2B SaaS, software development, fintech, e-commerce
- Companies actively hiring IT talent

---

## Why They're Unique

### Competitive Advantages
1. **Speed:** Places candidates in weeks, not months
2. **Quality:** Access to University of Belgrade talent (Top 500 worldwide)
3. **Cultural Fit:** Deep understanding of DACH business culture
4. **Cost:** Competitive rates compared to local DACH talent
5. **Scale:** Large active candidate pool (4,000+)

### Market Position
- Serving the undersupplied DACH IT talent market
- Solving the "open roles stay unfilled for months" problem
- Alternative to expensive internal recruiting teams

## Business Model

### Revenue Model

### Target Clients

## The Challenge (Why They Need Lead Gen)

### Primary Problem
- DACH companies don't know about Serbian IT talent pool
- Competing with local recruitment agencies
- Need to reach companies actively hiring (signal-based targeting)

### Our Solution
- Outbound B2B campaigns targeting DACH companies
- Signal-based targeting (active job postings 30+ days old)
- Multi-touch email sequences in English and German

---

## Campaign Focus

### Target Personas
1. **Primary:** HR Directors, Head of Talent (companies 100+ employees)
2. **Secondary:** CEOs, Founders (companies 50-100 employees)
3. **Tertiary:** CTOs, VPs of Engineering (as CC/warm intro)

### Buying Signals We Look For
- Active IT job postings 30+ days old
- Same job reposted multiple times (failed internal hiring)
- Hiring for internal recruiter (pipeline problem)
- Company growth 20%+ in last 6 months
- Recent funding (Series A/B)
- Job ads in multiple languages (looking internationally)

### Geographic Focus
- Germany (primary)
- Austria (primary)
- Switzerland (primary)

## Campaign Structure

### Lead Sourcing Stack
1. **Apollo:** Build company lists with job posting filters
2. **Clay:** Enrich, score signals, find decision makers
3. **SmartLead:** Campaign delivery and management

### Email Sequences
- 3-4 email sequence
- 2-day gaps between emails
- Stop on reply
- No tracking pixels

### Languages
- English (primary)
- German (for senior decision makers in DE/AT)

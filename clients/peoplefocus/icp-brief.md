## 4. Buying Signals & Targeting Logic

**CRITICAL:** Only contact companies that show at least **one Tier 1 signal**, or **two or more Tier 2/3 signals** combined.
**Do NOT spray — signal-based targeting only.**

### Tier 1 Signals (Highest Priority)

#### Active IT Job Postings
- Open IT roles on LinkedIn, Indeed, Xing, Stepstone posted **30+ days ago**
- Keywords: Software Engineer, Backend Developer, DevOps, Cloud Engineer, Java Developer, Python Developer, IT Manager
- **Same job reposted multiple times** — strong signal of failed internal hiring
- **Job ad available in multiple languages** — signal they are looking outside Germany/Austria/Switzerland

#### Posting for Internal Recruiter
- Company advertising for 'Talent Acquisition Manager' or 'IT Recruiter'
- They have a pipeline problem
- **Opportunity:** Position People Focus as faster & more cost-effective than building internal function

### Tier 2 Signals

#### Company Growth Signals
- LinkedIn headcount grew **20%+ in last 6 months**
- Recent **Series A or B funding** (last 12 months) — needs to scale team fast
- New office opening or new product line announced
- **New CTO or VP Engineering appointed** (last 90 days) — often triggers new hiring approach

#### Market Entry / Expansion
- DACH company expanding to new European market
- Recent press release about new product or partnership requiring tech build-out

### Tier 3 Signals

#### Frustration Signals
- Glassdoor / Kununu reviews mentioning 'understaffed', 'too slow to hire', 'overworked team'
- CTO or CEO LinkedIn post about hiring challenges or talent shortage
- **Job ad older than 90 days still active** — high frustration signal

|----------------|---------|

| **LinkedIn Headcount Growth** | Filter companies that grew 20%+ in last 6 months |

| **Contact Finder** | Find HR Director / Head of Talent / CEO — verify email via Prospeo or Hunter waterfall |

## 6. Value Proposition & Messaging

### Core Value Proposition
People Focus connects DACH companies with vetted senior IT talent from Serbia — **in weeks, not months**.

### Key Differentiators
- 4,000+ active candidates in database (last 2 years)
- 1,000+ candidate interviews per year
- 20+ years of management experience
- Deep understanding of DACH business culture
- Active DACH client base
- University of Belgrade — Top 500 Worldwide (Shanghai Ranking)

### Primary Pain Addressed
DACH IT talent market is undersupplied — open roles stay unfilled for months. People Focus solves this with access to Serbia's strong IT talent pool at competitive rates.

### Call to Action
- Book a 20-minute intro call
- OR reply to learn more

### Tone & Style
- Professional, direct, consultative — not salesy
- Mirror DACH business communication style (formal but efficient)

### Language Strategy
- **English:** Primary language for all outreach
- **German:** Version for senior decision makers (CEO/Geschäftsführer) in DE/AT

## 7. Exclusion Criteria (Do Not Contact)

**NEVER contact:**
- Recruitment agencies, headhunters, staffing firms (direct competitors)
- Companies with fewer than 50 employees
- Companies with no visible tech team or IT roles
- Companies outside DACH (unless explicitly approved)
- Existing People Focus clients
- Companies that have responded negatively or unsubscribed

---

## 8. Campaign Strategy

### Target Volume
- Start with signal-based list (not spray approach)
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Prioritize Tier 1 signals

### Sequence Structure
- 3-4 email follow-up sequence
- 2-day gaps between emails
- Stop on reply
- No tracking pixels (better deliverability)

### Personalization Strategy
- Reference specific job posting (if Tier 1 signal)
- Mention growth signals (funding, headcount increase)
- Reference frustration signals (long-open positions)
- Keep formal but human tone

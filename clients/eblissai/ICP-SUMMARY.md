# eBliss AI - ICP Summary

**Client:** eBliss AI (via INTELSOL)
**Service:** Autonomous Execution Layer for endpoint IT issues
**Date:** September 9, 2026
**Status:** Active - Just came out of stealth

---

## Core ICP (Quick Reference)

### Must Have:
1. **3,000+ endpoints** (lowered from 5,000+ in v6a update)
2. **Struggling with L2/L3 ticket backlog**
3. **Existing DEX/ITSM tools** that flag problems but don't fix them
4. **Mid to large enterprises** (added mid-market in v6a)
5. **High MTTR** and manual, repetitive investigation

### Sweet Spot:
- **10,000 +/- employees**
- **Fortune 500 or Global Top 5000**
- Already running **ServiceNow or BMC Helix**
- Active **L1/L2 support hiring** (signals pain)

## Buyer Personas (Priority Order)

### 1. Digital Workplace / EUC (PRIMARY)
- EVP/SVP/VP/Director/Head of End User Computing, Digital Workplace
- **Pain:** Cost per ticket, MTTR, patch compliance
- **Entry point for most deals**

### 2. Infrastructure & Operations - I&O (SECONDARY)
- EVP/SVP/VP/Director/Head of Infrastructure & Operations
- **Pain:** Aggregate IT opex, audit/compliance findings, ticket volume
- **Can refer down to Digital Workplace**

### 3. CTO / Chief AI Officer (SECONDARY - Signal-Based Only)
- CTO / Chief AI Officer, Chief Architect
- **Pain:** AI transformation progress, pilot-to-production conversion
- **Only engage when AI mandate signal exists**

### 4. Service Desk / Incident Management (TERTIARY)
- VP/Director of Service Desk, Incident Management Lead
- **Pain:** Ticket throughput, SLA adherence, escalation rate
- **Only when L2 work sits in Service Desk**

## Buying Signals

### Tier 1 (Strongest):
- **Deflection/triage AI already deployed** (Moveworks, Aisera, ServiceNow Now Assist) - treat as warm expansion, not competitor
- **DEX/EUM tool deployed** (Nexthink, Lakeside SysTrack) - signals budget + acknowledged pain

### Tier 2:
- **Hiring for Digital Workplace/EUC roles**
- **New VP/Director of EUC or I&O** (last 6 months)
- **Change of CIO or end user computing head** (NEW in v6a)
- **Ticket volume / IT headcount mismatch**

### Tier 3:
- **5,000+ endpoints** with favorable endpoint-to-headcount ratio

## Key Messaging

### Product Positioning (v6a Update):
- ✅ **"Autonomous Execution Layer"** (not "AI Workforce solution")
- ✅ Diagnoses, resolves endpoint issues **AND performs novel, complex work**
- ✅ For **mid and large enterprises** (expanded market)
- ✅ Close gap between flagging problems and fixing them
- ✅ **Prevent productivity AND revenue loss** (revenue angle NEW)

### Use These Terms:
- "AI Workforce" (trained and directed like new hire)
- "Diagnose / Resolve / Detect" for execs
- "Supervisory Agent" and "Static Agent"
- Autonomy ladder: supervised → semi-autonomous → fully autonomous → proactive autonomous
- **Avoid "replace" language** - position as leverage, not headcount reduction

### Don't Use:
- Model names (e.g., "Claude") in customer copy
- Claims beyond what's shipped

# TS Lab - ICP Documentation

**Client Website:** [tslab.eu](https://tslab.eu)
**Date:** 2026-05-20 (Updated after Matej call)

---

## Client Overview

TS Lab is a Slovenia-based food supplement capsule manufacturer. They produce capsules for other companies (B2B white-label manufacturing).

---

## Target Company Profile

### Target Companies

**MUST HAVE:**
- Sells food supplements
- Products are in capsule/tablet form (NOT only powders or bars)
- 20+ employees
- Online store or retail shop
- Multiple supplement brands
- NOT a private label (PL) manufacturing facility
- NOT a white label (WL) manufacturing facility

**NOTE:** Companies with their own manufacturing facility are NOT disqualified - they may need additional capacity.

**DISQUALIFIERS:**
- Only sells protein powders or bars (no capsules)
- Private label (PL) manufacturing facilities
- White label (WL) manufacturing facilities
- Only pharmacy selling prescription drugs
- MLM company
- Website is old or broken
- Under 20 employees
- Outside EU/UK

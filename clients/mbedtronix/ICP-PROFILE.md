# MBEDTRONIX - Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Client:** MBEDTRONIX
**Website:** mbedtronix.com
**Location:** Krusevac, Serbia
**Target Market:** Slovenia

---

## Service Overview

Nearshore development partner for electronics and embedded systems, covering the full hardware development cycle.

### Key Services

**Embedded Development:**
- Custom firmware
- Embedded software engineering
- IoT integration (device-to-cloud)
- Firmware optimization & debugging
- HAL layer development

**Electronics Development:**
- PCB design & schematic capture
- Hardware prototyping
- Component selection & sourcing
- Circuit simulation
- Product testing & validation

### Nearshore Positioning for Slovenia
- Geographic proximity (~4h drive from Slovenia)
- Language and cultural similarity
- Lower hourly rates than Slovenian/Western agencies
- HW + FW combined under one roof

---

## Core Problem Solved

Slovenian companies building physical products with electronics face:
- Shortage of embedded engineers (shallow, expensive, slow-to-hire talent market)
- Fluctuating development needs that don't justify a full-time hire
- Gap between idea and prototype for companies without in-house electronics team
- Pressure to add connectivity/"smart" features to existing products

---

## Target Geography

**Primary:** Slovenia

**Exclude:**
- Pure software companies with no hardware component
- Distributors/resellers of electronics with no in-house development
- Contract manufacturers (EMS) with no own products (unless they offer design services)
- Corporations with large in-house R&D departments (>250 employees) - long cycles, procurement barriers
- Micro companies with no product and no funding

---

## Target Segments

### Segment A - Manufacturers with Own Product (PRIMARY)

Companies that manufacture and sell their own physical products with an electronic component, or the potential for one.

**Firmographics:**
- Industries (SKD): C26 (electronic components), C27 (electrical equipment), C28 (machinery & equipment)
- Company size: 10-250 employees
- Revenue: EUR 1M-50M
- Signal: Own product (not purely contract manufacturing), in-house development team of up to 10 people

**Typical Examples:**
- Manufacturers of measurement equipment
- Electric motors & drives
- Heating/cooling technology
- Lighting
- Agricultural & forestry equipment
- Cleaning equipment

**Why They Buy:**
In-house development is overloaded or doesn't exist; MBEDTRONIX takes on the firmware/PCB part of the project.

---

### Segment B - Hardware & IoT Start-ups / Scale-ups

Young companies building a connected product (sensors, wearables, smart devices, agritech, energy).

**Firmographics:**
- Company size: 2-30 employees
- Stage: Seed to Series A, or backed by EU/national (SPS/SID) funding
- Signal: Product at prototype or pre-series production stage

**Why They Buy:**
Can't afford a full in-house hardware team; need a fast path from concept to validated prototype.

---

### Segment C - Industrial Automation & Integrators

Companies developing automation, machine vision, robotics and industrial IoT solutions for third-party clients.

**Firmographics:**
- Company size: 5-100 employees
- Signal: Project-based work, custom industrial solutions

**Why They Buy:**
Overflow capacity - larger projects need extra embedded developers without hiring.

---

### Segment D - Medtech / Specialized Electronics (SECONDARY)

Developers of medical and lab devices and specialized measurement technology. Higher project value, but longer sales cycles and regulatory requirements - suited to smaller, more personalized outreach rather than volume cold email.

## Decision Maker Personas

**For companies <30 employees:** Target the director directly
**For companies >30 employees:** Target the head of development/CTO

| Persona | Title Examples | When to Target |
|---------|---------------|----------------|
| **Primary: Technical Lead** | CTO, Technical Director, Head of Development, Head of R&D | Main decision-maker - deadlines, capacity, code quality |
| **Primary: Director** | Director, CEO (companies <30 employees) | Decision-maker / signatory - cost vs. hiring, time-to-market |
| **Secondary: Product Lead** | Project Manager, Product Manager | Influencer - feasibility, roadmap |

---

## Buying Signals & Targeting Logic

Use signals to prioritize which fit-matching prospects go into outreach first.

| Tier | Signal | What to Look For |
|------|--------|------------------|
| **Tier 1** | Active hiring | Open job postings for embedded/firmware/electronics engineers - the strongest signal: they're looking for capacity they can't find |
| **Tier 2** | Development funding secured | EU or national funding for development/digitalization (SPS vouchers, Horizon, public MGTS grants) |
| **Tier 2** | Product launch | New product launch or announcement of a "smart" version of an existing one |
| **Tier 3** | Trade fairs | Exhibiting at IFAM Ljubljana, MOS, embedded world and similar fairs |
| **Tier 3** | Growth / expansion | News about growth, investment or production expansion |

## How to Identify Target Companies

**Firmographic Fit:**
- Bizi.si / AJPES - filtered by SKD codes C26, C27, C28 + company size + revenue (Segment A)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator - titles "Head of Development", "CTO", "Technical Director" in electronics/machinery/automation, location Slovenia

**Trigger Sources:**
- Job boards (MojeDelo, Optius, LinkedIn Jobs) for Tier 1 signal (active embedded/firmware job postings)
- Lists of SPS funding recipients
- Exhibitor lists for IFAM Ljubljana and embedded world

**Decision-Maker Contacts:**
Technical lead/CTO or director (companies <30 employees) per Decision Maker Personas section

**Prioritization:**
- Companies with Tier 1 signal (active embedded/firmware job posting) go into outreach first
- Segments A and C are primary priority
- Segment B secondary
- Segment D smaller, more personalized outreach

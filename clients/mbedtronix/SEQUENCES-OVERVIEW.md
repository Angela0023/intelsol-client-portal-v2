# MBEDTRONIX Email Sequences - Master Overview

**Campaign:** Slovenian B2B Outbound for Embedded & Electronics Development Services
**Total Sequences:** 5 (4 segments + 1 tier-based)

---

## Sequence Prioritization

### Priority 1: TIER 1 - Active Hiring Signal
**File:** `TIER-1-ACTIVE-HIRING.md`
**Target:** ANY company with active job postings for embedded/firmware/electronics engineers
**Signal:** Job boards (MojeDelo, Optius, LinkedIn Jobs), company careers pages
**Why First:** Demonstrated need - they're actively trying to hire and failing
**Expected Volume:** Medium (10-30 companies/month)

### Priority 2: SEGMENT A - Manufacturers
**File:** `SEGMENT-A-MANUFACTURERS.md`
**Target:** Slovenian manufacturers (10-250 employees) with own electronic products
**Industries:** C26 (electronic components), C27 (electrical equipment), C28 (machinery & equipment)
**Why Second:** PRIMARY segment, largest addressable market
**Expected Volume:** High (50-100 companies/month)

### Priority 3: SEGMENT C - Industrial Automation
**File:** `SEGMENT-C-AUTOMATION.md`
**Target:** Automation, machine vision, robotics, industrial IoT integrators (5-100 employees)
**Why Third:** PRIMARY segment, project-based overflow needs
**Expected Volume:** Medium (20-40 companies/month)

### Priority 4: SEGMENT B - Hardware/IoT Startups
**File:** `SEGMENT-B-STARTUPS.md`
**Target:** Seed to Series A hardware startups (2-30 employees)
**Why Fourth:** SECONDARY segment, smaller volume but high-intent
**Expected Volume:** Low-Medium (10-20 companies/month)

### Priority 5: SEGMENT D - Medtech
**File:** `SEGMENT-D-MEDTECH.md`
**Target:** Medical devices, lab equipment, specialized measurement (10-100 employees)
**Why Last:** SECONDARY segment, highly personalized, low volume, longer sales cycles
**Expected Volume:** Very Low (5-10 companies/month, highly targeted)
**Special Note:** Requires deep research before sending, more consultative approach

## Sequence Structure

All sequences follow 3-email cadence:
- **Email 1:** Initial outreach (Day 0)
- **Email 2:** Follow-up 1 (Day 3-5)
- **Email 3:** Follow-up 2 (Day 6-10)

Exception: Segment D (Medtech) uses slower cadence (Day 5-7, Day 10-14)

## CTA Rules (Hormozi-Aligned)

**Every email ends with ONE clear CTA:**
- ❌ WRONG: "Let me know if you're interested"
- ❌ WRONG: "Feel free to reach out"
- ✅ CORRECT: "Worth 15 minutes this week?"
- ✅ CORRECT: "Open to a quick call?"
- ✅ CORRECT: "15 minutes work?"

**CTA Principles:**
- Specific time ask (15 minutes, 20 minutes, 30 minutes)
- Low commitment framing ("quick call", "brief conversation")
- Question format (invites response)
- NEVER multiple CTAs in one email

- Micro companies with no product/funding

# Intelsol ICP Documentation

**Client:** Matej Gostisa - Intelsol
**Service:** AI-powered outreach systems for B2B client acquisition
**Target Markets:** US + European B2B companies (DACH, France, Benelux, Nordics, UK)
**Campaign Start Date:** 2026-06-09

---

## Core ICP Criteria

### Must Have:
1. **B2B business model** - Sell to other companies, not consumers
2. **High-ticket offers** - Services/products valued at $20K+ per deal
3. **Active sales process** - Need to prospect and acquire new clients (not passive/distributor model)
4. **Growth-focused** - Open to systemizing their outbound

### Deal Breakers (Exclude):
- B2C companies
- Recruitment/staffing agencies (different pipeline problem)
- Companies selling through distributors only
- Commodity/low-margin businesses

---

## Industries to Include

### Primary (Professional Services):
- IT Services and IT Consulting
- Software Development
- Technology, Information and Internet
- Management Consulting
- Business Consulting Services

### Secondary (Manufacturing - High-Ticket B2B):

**INCLUDE these 8 manufacturing industries:**

1. Automation Machinery Manufacturing
2. Industrial Machinery Manufacturing
3. Commercial and Service Industry Machinery Manufacturing
4. Medical Equipment Manufacturing
5. Measuring and Control Instrument Manufacturing
6. Computer Hardware Manufacturing
7. Communications Equipment Manufacturing
8. Renewable Energy Equipment Manufacturing

**Pattern:** Manufacturing companies that sell expensive equipment/systems directly to other businesses and need active prospecting.

**EXCLUDE:** Consumer goods, commodity materials, component manufacturers selling to OEMs, low-ticket products.

## Company Size & Revenue

**Employee Count:** 20-200 employees
- Sweet spot: 50-100 employees
- Large enough to have budget, small enough to make decisions quickly

**Annual Revenue:** $10M - $100M
- High enough to afford high-ticket solutions
- Not so large they have mature SDR teams already

---

## Buyer Personas

**Primary Decision Makers:**
- CEO / Managing Director (companies <50 employees)
- VP of Sales / Head of Business Development
- Founder / Co-Founder (especially in tech/consulting)

**Secondary:**
- Director of Marketing (if sales-focused)
- Head of Growth

---

## Prospecting Signals (Good-to-Have)

- Founder/senior team doing outreach personally on LinkedIn
- Company has grown but sales process hasn't scaled
- Operates in multiple markets (US companies expanding internationally, EU companies needing multi-language outreach)
- High-value services but inconsistent pipeline
- Recently hired sales team but no SDR infrastructure

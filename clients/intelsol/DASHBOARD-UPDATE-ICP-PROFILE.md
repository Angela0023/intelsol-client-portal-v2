## Overview

This updated ICP profile shifts focus from the original IT/software-heavy approach to prioritize manufacturing companies (40% of volume) while tightening company size requirements based on Matej's guidance.

**Key Changes from Original ICP:**
- ✅ 40% focus on manufacturing companies (like TS Lab - best client)
- ✅ Added cosmetics/personal care and beverage manufacturing
- ✅ Added tier 1 & tier 2 suppliers (automotive, industrial components)
- ✅ Added staffing/recruiting as target industry (People Focus example)
- ✅ Added AI companies (5% - hungry for leads but challenging to deliver)
- ✅ Removed marketing agencies from targeting (meta ads, Google ads, SEO, WordPress)
- ✅ Keep wholesale consumer goods (B2B wholesale is good, B2C retail is excluded)
- ✅ Tightened employee range: 10-100 (max 150 for manufacturing only)
- ✅ Target companies doing sales manually ("not advanced in IT")
- ✅ 60% US market, 40% European markets

## ICP Verification Prompt (For Anthropic AI in Clay)

```
Analyze this company's website and determine if they match Intelsol's ICP for lead generation services.

A STRONG MATCH should be:
- B2B company selling to other businesses (NOT consumers)
- One of the following:
  * Manufacturing company (industrial machinery, medical equipment, automation, packaging, food/beverage manufacturing, consumer goods manufacturing, cosmetics/personal care, chemicals, plastics, tier 1/2 suppliers to OEMs, etc.)
  * Specialized B2B service company (creative/design agency, healthcare tech services, specialized consulting, logistics, staffing/recruiting, niche professional services, etc.)
  * Distribution/wholesale company (B2B distribution/wholesale of industrial products, materials, equipment, consumer goods sold to retailers)
  * Niche B2B SaaS (vertical SaaS, compliance software, industry-specific platforms)
  * AI companies (developing/selling AI products, founded 2022-2026, struggling with customer acquisition)
- 10-150 employees (sweet spot: 20-100)
- Needs active outbound sales process (not just inbound or passive distribution model)
- Has broad target market (many potential clients to reach)
- Located in: United States (primary), UK, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland (secondary)

STRONG positive signals (increase match score):
- Manufacturing company selling B2B products (especially cosmetics, food/beverage, consumer goods to distributors, tier 1/2 suppliers)
- Private label, white label, contract manufacturing, or OEM business model
- Tier 1 or tier 2 supplier to large manufacturers (automotive, industrial components)
- Staffing/recruiting company (IT staffing, manufacturing workers, specialized recruiting)
- AI company developing/selling AI products (founded recently, needs customer acquisition help)
- Growing company (website mentions scaling, expansion, hiring, growth)
- Founder or CEO still involved in sales (mentions "founder-led" or CEO bio talks about sales)
- Mentions "manual processes" or "traditional approach" on website
- Has strong products/services but website doesn't mention modern sales tools/SDR team
- Serves niche market within a large industry (specialized, not generic)
- Mentions "custom" or "tailored" solutions (indicates consultative sales approach)
- B2B wholesale including consumer goods sold to retailers (not B2C retail)

NOT a match if:
- B2C company (sells directly to consumers, has eCommerce for consumers, retail focus)
- Marketing agency (meta ads, Google ads, SEO services, WordPress development, website builders)
- Generic IT consulting (too saturated, avoid unless extremely niche)
- Managed services provider (MSP)
- Under 10 employees (too small to have budget for our services)
- Over 150 employees (likely already has sales infrastructure - SDR team, BDR team, etc.)
- Large enterprise with 200+ employees
- Website mentions "SDR team", "sales development team", "BDR team", or "director of sales operations" (already has sales infrastructure)
- Sells only through distributors with no direct sales process
- Broken website, very outdated website (over 5+ years old design), or no clear value proposition
- Non-English website with no presence in target markets (US/EU)

Output format:
- "Yes" or "No"
- 1-2 sentence explanation of why

Example output:
"Yes. Industrial automation equipment manufacturer with 65 employees selling custom automation systems to food & beverage companies across North America. B2B sales with broad target market and active outbound needed."

or

"No. Generic IT consulting firm with 250+ employees and website mentions they have an SDR team and sales operations manager, indicating they already have mature sales infrastructure."
```

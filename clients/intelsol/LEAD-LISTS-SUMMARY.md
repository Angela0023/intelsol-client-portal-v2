## Tag Column Explanation

Each lead has a "Tag" column with two values:
- **"Indian"** - Name appears to be Indian (for separate campaign targeting)
- **"Other"** - All other leads

**Why separate campaigns?**
Indian leads have shown higher response rates, so you can create targeted campaigns based on the Tag field.

## How to Use

### For List-1-Target-Now.csv:
1. **Import to SmartLead** or your campaign tool
2. **Create two campaigns:**
   - Campaign A: Filter by Tag = "Indian"
   - Campaign B: Filter by Tag = "Other"
3. **Target immediately** - these are your highest-priority decision makers

### For List-2-Remaining-Leads.csv:
1. **Use for follow-up campaigns** after List 1 is exhausted
2. **Same tag-based filtering** (Indian vs Other)
3. **Lower priority** but still valid leads from the same companies

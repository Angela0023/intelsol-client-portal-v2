## Updated ICP Verification Prompt (Quick Version)

```
Is this company a match for Intelsol?

Match =
- B2B (sells to businesses, not consumers)
- Manufacturing OR specialized services OR distribution OR niche SaaS
- 10-150 employees (sweet spot 20-100)
- High-ticket sales ($20K+ deals)
- US, UK, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Nordics
- Broad target market (lots of potential clients)

Strong signals:
- Manufacturing company
- Growing, scaling
- Founder-led sales
- "Manual processes" on website
- NOT mentioned: "SDR team" or "BDR team"

Not a match =
- B2C (consumers)
- Under 10 or over 150 employees
- Recruitment/staffing
- Generic IT consulting
- Has SDR/BDR team already
- Low-ticket products

Output: "Yes" or "No" + 1 sentence why
```

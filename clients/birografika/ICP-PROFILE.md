**TIER 2: Packaging & Technical**

- Evaluates on: reliability, capacity for growth, business partnership fit, logistics advantage

## Value Proposition

### TIER 1: Procurement & Purchasing
- **Cost efficiency:** Competitive label sourcing via nearshore economics (Serbia vs Western Europe)
- **Capacity & flexibility:** Handle multi-SKU programmes, seasonal volumes, portfolio scaling
- **Quality certifications:** ISO 9001, ISO 14001, FSC certified, SEDEX audited for second-source confidence
- **Supply reliability:** Shorter lead times from European base, consistent delivery to CEE/DACH markets
- **Technical fit:** Full pre-press support, material compatibility, production feasibility assessment

### TIER 2: Packaging & Technical
- **Production excellence:** Modern equipment (MPS EFC 430, Grafotronic, Rotoflex inspection) for consistent finish quality
- **Material flexibility:** Paper, OPP/BOPP, PP, PE, duplex/triplex films—full range of label substrates
- **Technical partnership:** Specification development, material trials, quality validation, artwork support
- **Certifications & compliance:** ISO, FSC, HACCP systems for quality and food-safe production
- **Finish capabilities:** Die-cutting, lamination, cold foil, embossing, specialty effects

### TIER 3: Operations & Leadership
- **Reliable European partner:** 60+ years of manufacturing stability, family-owned commitment to long-term relationships
- **Production capacity:** Proven ability to handle recurring volumes and growth across FMCG segments
- **Nearshore logistics:** Short lead times and cost advantage vs Western Europe, strategic alternative to higher-cost local production
- **Direct partnership:** No intermediaries, direct relationship with decision-makers and production management
- **Portfolio support:** Flexibility to support new products, seasonal campaigns, and multi-site rollouts

## Key Differentiators

1. **European-Based Manufacturing** - Local production with shorter lead times
2. **Niche Label Expertise** - Specialized in pressure-sensitive and roll labels for FMCG
3. **Quality Certifications** - ISO and FSC certified for premium and sustainable brands
4. **Design-Focused Support** - Pre-press and artwork assistance for brand presentation
5. **Competitive Economics** - Cost-effective label production without quality compromise

## EMAIL SEQUENCE STRATEGY

**Sequence Structure:** Role-based (3 Tiers)

**Number of Sequences:** 3

**TIER 1: SEQUENCE A - Procurement & Purchasing**
- **Target Personas:** Procurement Manager, Head of Purchasing, Strategic Buyer, Packaging Buyer
- **Messaging Angle:** Cost comparison, nearshore economics, landed-cost advantage, supply reliability, second-source evaluation
- **Decision Criteria:** Evaluate based on cost, capacity, technical specifications, delivery reliability, quality certifications
- **Email Focus:** "Cost-competitive European label sourcing with capacity and quality"
- **Key Messages:** Landed-cost advantage, nearshore economics, production capacity, ISO/FSC certifications, multi-SKU capability

**TIER 2: SEQUENCE B - Packaging & Technical**
- **Target Personas:** Packaging Manager, Head of Packaging, Technical Manager, Quality Manager
- **Messaging Angle:** Technical capabilities, quality standards, production consistency, finish quality, specifications support, trials and approval
- **Decision Criteria:** Evaluate based on technical fit, production quality, material compatibility, pre-press support, trial management
- **Email Focus:** "Technical excellence and production consistency for label specifications"
- **Key Messages:** Production equipment (MPS EFC 430, Grafotronic, Rotoflex), material options (paper, OPP/BOPP, PP, PE), quality systems, pre-press support

**TIER 3: SEQUENCE C - Operations & Leadership**
- **Target Personas:** Supply Chain Manager, Operations Director, Production Manager, CEO/Owner, Managing Director
- **Messaging Angle:** Partnership and reliability, production capacity, recurring supply, nearshore logistics, business partnership, volume planning
- **Decision Criteria:** Evaluate based on partnership fit, reliability, capacity for growth, direct relationship, long-term stability
- **Email Focus:** "Reliable European manufacturing partner for recurring label volumes"
- **Key Messages:** 60+ years of manufacturing, family-owned stability, European partnership, volume flexibility, nearshore logistics advantage

**Rationale:**
Three role-based sequences align messaging with distinct buying motivations:
- **TIER 1** focuses on procurement's cost and supply optimization
- **TIER 2** focuses on packaging/technical's specification and quality requirements
- **TIER 3** focuses on operations/leadership's partnership and reliability needs

This structure is simpler to manage (3 sequences vs 6), more focused messaging, and maps directly to the three primary buyer personas documented in the Positions CSV.
